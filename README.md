Two lines to a past self. Line one warns about one specific moment; line two says why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. The program writes the two lines to `entry.txt`.

> Don't delete the voicemail your grandmother left on your birthday.
> It is the only recording of her voice you will ever have, and you will replay it every birthday after she is gone.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
